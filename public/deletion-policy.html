Account & Data Deletion Policy

At RichaSales, we respect your privacy and your right to control your personal information. This policy explains how you can request the deletion of your account and associated data from our platform.

Who Can Request Deletion?

Any registered customer using RichaSales may request the permanent deletion of their account and related information at any time.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request account deletion, please send an email from your registered email address to:

Email: support@richasales.com

Please include the following information:

  • Your registered email address.
  • Your account or customer name.
  • A request stating that you wish to permanently delete your account.

What Data Will Be Deleted?

Once your deletion request is approved, we will permanently remove your account and personal information, including:

  • Account profile information
  • AI agent configurations
  • Call history and activity logs
  • Purchased AI calling minutes
  • Customer contact information stored within the platform
  • Associated usage records where applicable

Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained where required by applicable laws, regulatory requirements, fraud prevention, security purposes, or financial record-keeping obligations. Any retained data will only be kept for the minimum period required by law.

Deletion Processing Time

After receiving your request and verifying ownership of the account, your account deletion request will normally be completed within 7 business days.

Important Notice

Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone. After deletion, you will lose access to your purchased AI calling minutes, AI agents, call history, and all associated platform data.

Need Help?

If you have any questions regarding this policy or need assistance with your deletion request, please contact us:

Website: https://richasales.com/
Email: support@richasales.com
